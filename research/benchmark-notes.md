## Benchmark Patterns

The strongest robotics and STEM team sites tend to include:

- An immediate mission statement and clear call to join or sponsor.
- Team history and values, especially FIRST-style gracious professionalism.
- Program pathways for mechanical, software, strategy, business, and outreach.
- Robot archive pages that preserve institutional memory.
- Sponsor tiers with concrete benefits.
- Events and outreach pages that show community impact.
- Media galleries and competition storytelling.
- Lightweight admin/content workflows so students can maintain the site.

Benchmarks reviewed included Team 254, Stormgears FRC 5422, FRC 2813 Gear Heads, Team 751 barn2robotics, FRC Team 180 S.P.A.M., PCHS Robotics, Team 1661, Team 4272 Maverick Robotics, Washburn Millerbots, and FIRST/WPILib ecosystem pages.
